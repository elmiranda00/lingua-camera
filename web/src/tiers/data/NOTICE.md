# Word list credits and licences

These two files are generated by `npm run wordlists` (`scripts/build-wordlists.mjs`) from the
sources below. Source files are checked against pinned SHA-256 hashes.

## `fr-common.json`: licence CC BY-SA 4.0

All inflected forms of the 2,000 most frequent French lemmas, ranked by film-subtitle plus book frequency.

- **Source:** Lexique 3.83, <https://www.lexique.org/databases/Lexique383/>
- **Authors:** Boris New, Christophe Pallier, Marc Brysbaert, Ludovic Ferrand.
  New, B., Pallier, C., Brysbaert, M., Ferrand, L. (2004). *Lexique 2: A new French lexical database.*
  Behavior Research Methods, Instruments, & Computers, 36(3), 516–524.
- **Licence:** [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- **Changes made:** reduced to the forms of the 2,000 most frequent lemmas (frequency =
  `freqlemfilms2` + `freqlemlivres`), lower-cased, Unicode NFC, one entry per form, JSON array.
- **Share-alike:** this file, and any adaptation of it, stays under CC BY-SA 4.0. The rest of the
  app uses it only as data.

## `zh-hsk12.json`: MIT (builder) over the official HSK 3.0 word standard

The 1,256 simplified-Chinese words of HSK 3.0 levels 1 and 2.

- **Source:** `wordlists/inclusive/new/2.json` from
  [drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary](https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary)
  at commit `7ac65bf1a6387d35f1ade478906172a19311c7f9`.
  Copyright (c) 2026 Yanis Zafirópulos (Dr.Kameleon), MIT licence.
- **Upstream:** that repository takes its HSK 3.0 lists from
  [elkmovie/hsk30](https://github.com/elkmovie/hsk30). The word lists come from the official
  HSK standard; upstream licences are not stated by the repository, so only the word forms are used.
- **Changes made:** only the `simplified` field is kept, de-duplicated and sorted.
